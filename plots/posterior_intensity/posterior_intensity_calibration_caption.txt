Recovery of the structured log-intensity under increasing latent overdispersion. Counts were generated for 100 individuals at 128 grid points according to Y_it | eta_it ~ Poisson[exp(eta_it)], with eta_it = B_it + sigma*z_it, z_it ~ N(0,1), and sigma in {0, 0.5, 1, 2}. The same genotype matrix, 2 causal variables, structured log-intensity B, and standardized latent deviations z were used in every panel. Gray points are the observed log1p counts, blue circles are posterior means of B from Poisson fSuSiE, and orange triangles are fitted values from Gaussian fSuSiE applied to log1p counts. The dashed line is the identity line; colored solid lines are least-squares calibration lines. Panel annotations report the calibration intercept, slope, and root mean squared error, computed over all individual-grid pairs. From sigma = 0 to sigma = 2, the calibration slope changed from 0.98 to 0.94 for Poisson fSuSiE and from 0.88 to 0.73 for fSuSiE on log1p counts; the corresponding calibration intercepts changed from 0.06 to 0.07 and from 0.38 to 0.89, respectively. The common plotting range contains 99.9% of observed log1p counts; clipping is for display only, and all values were used to compute the calibration statistics.
